01 / Our storyFrom magazine to publishing

It started
with friends.

And a belief that what we make matters.

We found each other at house shows, warehouse raves, film screenings, zine launches, and our local dive, Tweedy’s.

We shared artistic goals, but needed a meaningful place to showcase our work and the community around it. So we started Locket Magazine: a place to document and celebrate Austin’s creative community.

As we’ve grown, so has our world. We’ve encountered projects, people, and mediums beyond the magazine’s pages. Locket Publishing is our next step: sharing our team, resources, and what we’ve learned to help others develop and launch independent publications.

Basically, we want more magazines. And more opportunities to collaborate with people making work we love.
