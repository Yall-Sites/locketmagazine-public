Locket Publishing is

A home for
interdependent
media.

From the community making Locket Magazine.

A little over a year ago, we launched Locket Issue 001: a snapshot of our friends creating, living and having fun in Austin, TX. Amidst the city’s quick and unsightly shifts, we believed our creative community deserved to be documented and celebrated.

Since the beginning, we’ve wanted to develop publications as distinctive and varied as our creative communities in Austin, and beyond. After selling out three issues, hosting events with 500+ attendees, raising thousands in funds for immigration legal services, and launching a digital publication this past year, we’re set to print more.

Essentially, we want more magazines. And more opportunities to collaborate with people producing work we love. Locket Publishing is our next step in growing as a resource and home for independent media.

01 / History

Locket Magazine is

Created by
a group of
friends.

We found each other playing house shows, DJing raves, screening films, and curating exhibitions. We hung out at parties, sandlot baseball games, vintage markets and Tweedy’s. Though we shared creative goals, we lacked a meaningful place to showcase our work and the community around it. So we started Locket Magazine: an outlet by and for friends making it happen in Austin, TX.

Our goal is to create a document of the artists in our lives by capturing the people, places and moments shaping them. It’s our physical proof that there is quite a lot going on here. We think we owe it to each other to capture it.
